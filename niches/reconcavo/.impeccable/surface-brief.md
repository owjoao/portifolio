# Surface Brief — Recôncavo Culinária

## Scope and visitor mode

Surface: public restaurant presentation site (`index.html`).
Mode: Persuade — the visitor should understand the restaurant, find the current menu and choose a contact action quickly.
Audience: people considering a meal or visit in Barra, Salvador, and guests looking for the current digital menu.

## Confirmed brief

- Priority action: view the existing Strelo menu.
- Secondary actions: contact by WhatsApp or phone, find the address and view public information.
- No login, password, checkout, dashboard or invented menu data.
- Use the supplied logo, business details, menu URL and real supplied photography.
- The visual reference informs rhythm and hierarchy only; do not copy its identity, copy, images or exact layout.
- Build a premium Brazilian gastronomic brand with a contemporary Bahian point of view.
- Keep cultural references tactile and subtle: bead texture, wood, ceramics, music, coast and warm indirect light.

## Direction contract

### Thesis

A restaurant presentation should feel like a beautifully edited menu book: a clear path to the current menu, with the warmth of a memorable meal and the confidence of a contemporary Bahian brand.

### Own-world

Cream paper and deep root-green surfaces establish contrast. Saffron and terracotta carry the energy of the logo and the food. Arched image frames, editorial serif typography, fine rules, numbered sections and real table imagery make the page feel premium without becoming decorative or tourist-oriented.

### Story

The visitor first sees the name and the promise “Sabores que nascem do Recôncavo”, then the identity, the menu, the cultural atmosphere, the gallery and practical visit information. The menu CTA and WhatsApp action remain visible at every major decision point.

### First viewport

Desktop: a slim dark-green utility bar, compact navigation, a split hero with a large food/table composition and a prominent saffron menu action. Mobile: brand and menu button, a compact headline, stacked actions and the visual panel immediately below.

### Form

The signature is the arched editorial frame. It appears in the identity image, menu board, experience banner, map and final invitation. A saffron rule, section number and short metadata line keep the long page measured.

### Finish

The final pass includes HTML/JSON-LD validation, local asset checks, JavaScript syntax validation, desktop/mobile screenshots, keyboard-visible focus states, reduced-motion support and a clear handoff for GitHub Pages.
